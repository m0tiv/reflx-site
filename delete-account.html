ACCOUNT CONTROL

Delete your REFLX account

Request permanent deletion of your account and associated cloud data.

How to request deletion

Email reflx.support@proton.me from the email address connected to your secured REFLX account. Use the subject REFLX account deletion request and include your current REFLX username.

Request account deletion

Data that will be deleted

After account ownership is verified, RELLI Interactive will delete the authentication account, email association, account identifier, cloud-saved progress, settings, competitive rating, and leaderboard records associated with the account.

Data that may be retained

Limited security, fraud-prevention, and legal records may be retained where reasonably necessary. Backup copies may remain temporarily until routine backup rotation is complete. Local progress stored on the device can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling REFLX.

Contact

Questions about account deletion can be sent to reflx.support@proton.me.
