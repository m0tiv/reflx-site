Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

Information we collect

REFLX stores gameplay progress, settings, player usernames, test scores, accuracy, reaction times, competitive ratings, and account identifiers needed to provide cloud saves and leaderboards. If you secure an account, your email address is processed by Supabase for authentication. Passwords are handled by Supabase and are not stored by REFLX.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the game, save progress, authenticate accounts, display competitive rankings, prevent abuse, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

Public information

Leaderboard entries may publicly display your chosen username, score, rank, level, rating, and related gameplay statistics. Do not use a username containing personal information you do not want to share.

Service providers and storage

Gameplay information is stored locally on your device and, when online features are used, in the REFLX Supabase project. Supabase processes authentication and hosted database information on our behalf. Basic network, security, and authentication logs may also be processed to operate and protect the service.

Information we do not collect

REFLX does not sell personal information, serve targeted advertising, or collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or files from your device.

Retention and deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated cloud data by emailing reflx.support@proton.me. Please contact us from the email connected to the account when possible. Some information may be retained temporarily where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or backup integrity.

Children

REFLX is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it when appropriate.

Changes

We may update this policy as REFLX changes. The effective date above will identify the latest revision.

Contact

Questions, privacy requests, and support inquiries can be sent to reflx.support@proton.me.
